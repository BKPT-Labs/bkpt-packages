BKPT candidate architecture policy, version2; independent review required before publication.
Scope: STM32C542RCT6/LQFP64, NUCLEO-C542RC nominal NUC542RC$KR1, HAL2 pack2.1.0 and captured c5-hal2-base foundation only. Board/silicon revision and electrical setup remain unverified.
Capacity1 is conservative exclusive ownership policy for a physical timer, channel, request line, IRQ, peripheral or pad. Alternatives use routes, never cloned physical capacity. Manufacturer restrictions require separate citations.
periodic_interrupt frequency_hz1..1000 remains implementation input. Timer card retains checked clock arithmetic, internal upcount, prescaler0..65534 and ARR1..65535; allocation does not prove exact rate or interrupt latency.
gpio_output supports reviewed PA5 baseline adoption or PA4 explicit configuration. PA5 cannot concurrently serve SPI1 SCK. Pin allocation does not prove LED wiring, load safety or external device select semantics.
serial_endpoint remains captured USART2 polling1152008N1. USART2/PA2/PA3 may instead be adopted once by a finite UART DMA TX, RX or duplex profile. Shared stdio must be serialized with application traffic.
DMA finite-block profiles support memory copies with equal1/2/4-byte widths, or UART/I2C/SPI byte transfers with explicitly allocated physical request/channel/IRQ paths. Block count1..65535 and source-width divisibility are checked; actual buffer alignment, bounds, address reachability/protection and lifetime remain application obligations.
I2C controller profile uses source-pinned144MHz/1MHz timing with electrical prerequisites, both DMA directions serialized, event/error IRQs and a hash-pinned ES0676 BERR workaround for interrupt controller modes only. Polling paths and arbitrary timing are outside this profile.
SPI controller profile uses144MHz/16,8bit mode0,full duplex/internal NSS and both DMA/peripheral IRQs. External select requires a separately allocated GPIO and device-specific polarity/timing evidence.
RM hardware support for peripheral-to-peripheral does not certify an arbitrary endpoint pair or two readiness handshakes. Linked/circular/triggered/low-power/security/cache profiles and complete topology remain outside executable coverage until independently reviewed implementations exist.
Keep baseline clock and SysTick. All capability domains remain open/incomplete. Do not block in IRQ callbacks. Resource allocation, materialization, compilation and hardware observation are distinct; no complete errata/electrical/application qualification is claimed.
